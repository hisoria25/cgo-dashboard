Unit Economics → BER

Every rule on the cheat sheet compares ROAS against your break-even ROAS. Campaigns named Product | BER | Margin use their own BER. Everything else falls back to this.

The maths behind every verdict

gross margin = (price − cost − shipping − fees) ÷ price

BER = 1 ÷ gross margin

profit margin = gross margin − (1 ÷ ROAS)

That last line is why the sheet holds together: ROAS above BER and profit margin above zero are the same statement. The +35% / +20% / hold ladder reads this number.
